Features

Everything a trader needs,
right in the corner.

StockPeek packs a full trading terminal into a tiny widget that never gets in your way.

📊
Live Charts
Interactive canvas charts with volume bars, previous close line, and OHLC tooltip. 1D · 1W · 1M · 6M · 1Y · All time ranges.
🔍
Any Ticker
Search any Yahoo Finance symbol — Indian stocks, US equities, crypto, ETFs, indices, mutual funds. Autocomplete with type badges.
🔔
Price Alerts
Set above/below price targets. Fetched directly from Yahoo Finance every 5 seconds — no service worker delay. Toast fires in under 5 seconds.
📈
Stats & News
52-week range, P/E, market cap, beta, dividend yield. Plus 6 latest headlines from Yahoo Finance, updated on demand.
🔄
Always Fresh
Chart auto-refreshes every 30 seconds. Also refreshes instantly when you hover if data is older than 30s — so you always see the latest price.
🌓
Light & Dark Theme
Toggle between dark and light themes with one click. Chart labels, grid, and tooltips all adapt — no white-on-white surprises.
How it works

Up in 30 seconds.

1
🧩
Install the extension
Add StockPeek from the Chrome Web Store. No account, no API key, no setup. Visit any webpage and the circle appears instantly.
2
🖱
Hover to expand
Move your cursor over the circle. It springs open to show the live price, % change, and a full interactive chart with tabs.
3
📉
Peek, don't switch tabs
Move away and it collapses. After 3 seconds it drifts to the corner. Drag it wherever you want — it stays there.

Price Alerts

Know the moment it moves.

Set a target price and StockPeek fetches the price directly from Yahoo Finance every 5 seconds — no service worker, no delays. When the price crosses your target, a sleek toast notification slides in on the page you're reading, in under 5 seconds.

  • Above or below any price target
  • Direct fetch — bypasses service worker entirely
  • Under 5 second delivery, consistently
  • In-page toast — no Chrome permissions needed
  • Alert auto-clears once triggered
  • Background fallback via chrome.alarms for other tickers
